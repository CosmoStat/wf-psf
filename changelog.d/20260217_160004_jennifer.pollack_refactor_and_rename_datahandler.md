### Breaking changes

- `DataHandler` is now deprecated and will be removed in a future
  release once all downstream consumers are updated. Use
  `SimulationDataLoader` or `TensorFlowDatasetConverter` directly.
  `DataHandlerFactory` added as single entry point for creating
  appropriate data loaders based on `data_type` configuration.
